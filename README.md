# AP Space Tech Connector 🚀🏛️

> **Government of Andhra Pradesh (GoAP)** — Official connector platform bridging India's premier commercial space tech companies (**Skyroot Aerospace**, **Dhruva Space**, **Agnikul Cosmos**, **Pixxel Space**) with high-precision Andhra Pradesh-based component suppliers and the proposed AP Space Park.

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **4 Prime Space Tech Company Grids**:
   - 🚀 **Skyroot Aerospace**: Vikram launch vehicle stage adapter rings, titanium engine mounts, filament-wound motor casings, and mobile transport rigs.
   - 🛰️ **Dhruva Space**: DSOD satellite deployers, space-grade avionics PCBAs, solar array deployment hinges, and telemetry transceivers.
   - 🔥 **Agnikul Cosmos**: Agnibaan rocket 3D engine post-processing, Inconel chambers, mobile launch pad substructures, and cryogenic valve manifolds.
   - 👁️ **Pixxel Space**: Hyperspectral camera optical benches, invar lens mounts, radiation-tolerant FPGA payload processors, and thermal radiators.

2. **AP Space Park Strategic Collaboration Pitch**:
   - Detailed rationale for why **Skyroot, Dhruva, Agnikul, and Pixxel** should set up assembly & testing hubs in AP (45 km proximity to Satish Dhawan Space Centre SHAR Sriharikota, subsidized ISO-5 cleanrooms, TVAC chambers, 50% capital subsidies, and single-window 14-day statutory approvals).

3. **Master Andhra Pradesh Space Supplier Directory**:
   - Filterable catalog of AS9100D & ISRO-qualified manufacturers across AP clusters (**Sri City**, **Tirupati**, **Visakhapatnam**, **Kurnool**, **Vijayawada**, **Anantapur**).
   - Live text search & capability dropdown filters.
   - Interactive Supplier Facility Modal showcasing equipment (5-axis CNCs, cleanrooms, TVACs) and contact info.

4. **AP MSME Vendor Registration & Empanelment**:
   - Online form for AP precision component suppliers to register for GoAP space taskforce empanelment with automatic tracking ID generation.
