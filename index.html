Strategic Value Propositions for Space Primes

State infrastructure backing, highway logistics, and regulatory incentives under the AP Aerospace & Defence Policy G.O. Ms No. 42.

Skyroot Aerospace: Launch Stage Integration Hub

45-Minute Highway Transit to SHAR:

Eliminate risk-prone interstate transport of Vikram-I rocket stages by establishing a final stage integration facility in Tirupati Space Park directly connected via SH-71.

Isolated Static Engine Test Facility:

AP Government offers isolated static engine test beds in Kurnool District with pre-cleared environmental & explosive handling permissions for Raman & Dhawan liquid engines.

50% Capital Subsidy on Additive Manufacturing:

Up to ₹15 Crore capital reimbursement for establishing high-throughput 3D metal printing facilities in Sri City under G.O. Ms No. 42.

METRIC: 70% REDUCTION IN LOGISTICS RISK

Dhruva Space: Satellite Assembly & TVAC Hub

Subsidized ISO Class 5 Cleanroom & TVAC Chamber:

Access AP Govt's shared Centre of Excellence in Tirupati equipped with 500 kg Thermal Vacuum Chambers (TVAC) and vibration shaker tables at subsidized operating tariffs.

Coastal Ground Station Telemetry Allotment:

Allotted coastal land in Nellore District with zero RF interference for Dhruva's expanding global Satellite Ground Station network.

Local Micro-Composite Supply Integration:

Direct supply chain integration with Ananth & Tirupati MSMEs for DSOD deployer manufacturing, cutting procurement lead times to under 14 days.

METRIC: ₹22 CR ANNUAL TESTING SAVINGS

Agnikul Cosmos: Mobile Launch Pad & Finishing Hub

Dhanush Mobile Launch Pad Staging Corridor:

Dedicated coastal land in Sri City / Krishnapatnam port corridor for staging, testing, and rapid deployment of mobile launch systems to SHAR.

Post-Processing Center at Siemens COE:

Utilize AP Government's Dassault & Siemens CoE in Visakhapatnam for automated 5-axis CNC finishing and non-destructive CT scanning of Agnilet 3D engines.

14-Day Fast-Track Single Window Approvals:

Single-window statutory clearance for liquid oxygen (LOX), kerosene propellant storage, and hazardous chemical handling permits.

METRIC: 2-WEEK RAPID LAUNCH DEPLOYMENT

Pixxel Space: Optics Cleanroom & State Data Partnership

Precision Optics Cleanrooms in Sri City:

Specialized optical alignment cleanrooms with vibration isolation optical tables designed for ultra-sensitive hyperspectral camera assembly.

AP Anchor Earth Observation Data Partnership:

Government of Andhra Pradesh as an anchor customer for Pixxel's Fireflies hyperspectral imagery for agriculture, water resources, and disaster management.

R&D Talent Pipeline from IIT Tirupati:

Direct sponsorship of specialized Master's programs in Remote Sensing & Electro-Optics to provide continuous talent for Pixxel's AP facility.

METRIC: GUARANTEED ANCHOR STATE REVENUE
